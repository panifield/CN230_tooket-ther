# Design System: Together AI (Coastal Edition)

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
A "Coastal-Native Cloud" aesthetic. It maintains high-precision typography and enterprise-grade density but uses a palette of sun-bleached blues, soft sands, and organic brown accents.

**Key Characteristics:**
* **Aqueous Gradients:** Transitions from sky blue (#AAD6FA) to pale cream (#FCE6A9).
* **The "Midnight" Anchor:** Deep Blue-Black (#010120) for primary actions and footers.
* **Organic Grounding:** Driftwood Brown (#6f5e4e) for structural metadata.
* **Geometric Precision:** Sharp 4px corners and aggressive negative letter-spacing (-1.92px to -0.8px).

---

## 2. Color Palette & Roles

### Brand Colors
* **Primary Blue (#AAD6FA):** Lead brand color. Used for primary icons and solid secondary buttons.
* **Accent Yellow (#FCE6A9):** Secondary brand color. Used for warm highlights and gradients.
* **Driftwood Brown (#6f5e4e):** 🆕 Used for upper-corner labels and structural metadata (e.g., "REFUND / BKG-ID") to add a grounded, organic feel.

### Surfaces & Accents
* **Cream Base (#FFF4C7):** Warm background for banners or subtle sections.
* **Sky Tint (#C5F6FA):** Soft backgrounds and secondary UI elements.
* **Dark Blue (#010120):** Primary dark surface for buttons, footers, and high-contrast text.
* **Pure White (#FFFFFF):** Standard canvas background.

---

## 3. Typography Rules
* **Headlines (The Future):** 500 Weight | Negative Spacing (-0.8px to -1.92px).
* **Labels (PP Neue Montreal Mono):** 500 Weight | Uppercase | Spacing (+0.055px).

---

## 4. Component Stylings

### Buttons
* **Primary (Dark):** Background: `#010120` | Text: `#FFFFFF` | Radius: 4px.
* **Secondary (Solid Blue):** 🆕 Background: `#AAD6FA` | Text: `#010120` | Radius: 4px | Border: None.
* **Action (Ghost):** Background: `rgba(170, 214, 250, 0.2)` | Border: 1px solid `#AAD6FA`.

### Cards
* **Surface:** White or Cream Base.
* **Radius:** 8px (Container) / 4px (Internal elements).
* **Shadow:** `rgba(1, 1, 32, 0.06) 0px 4px 12px` (Blue-tinted soft shadow).

---

## 5. Do’s and Don’ts

### ✅ Do
* Use **Driftwood Brown (#6f5e4e)** for metadata labels in the upper corners. #6f5e4e
* Keep **The Future** headlines dense and tightly tracked.
* Use **Dark Blue (#010120)** for the footer to anchor the design. #010120

### ❌ Don't
* **Don't** use pure black for text or shadows; use Dark Blue or Blue-tinted shadows.
* **Don't** use rounded/pill shapes; keep corners at 4px.
